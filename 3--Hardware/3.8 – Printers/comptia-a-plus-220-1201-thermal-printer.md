## How Thermal Printers Work

A thermal printer creates output by applying **heat** to specially coated paper — no ink, no toner required.

- Areas of the paper that receive heat **turn black**
- Areas that do not receive heat remain white
- The output is created entirely by the chemical reaction in the paper coating

**Common applications:**
- Store receipts (cash registers, credit card terminals)
- Shipping labels (package delivery)
- Ticket printing

**Advantages:**
- No ink or toner required — lower ongoing supply cost
- Nearly silent operation — only the feed motor is audible
- Simple construction with few components

---

## Feed Assembly

The feed assembly moves the thermal paper through the printer past the heating element.

- Uses **friction** to grip and advance the paper (no complex paper path mechanism)
- Driven by a **feed roller** turned by a gear/motor
- Thermal paper typically comes in a **roll** that sits inside the printer

---

## Heating Element

The heating element is the core printing component.

- Extends the **full width of the printing area** — one fixed piece
- **Does not move** — the paper moves past it
- Heat is applied to specific points along the element as paper passes, creating the printed pattern

Because the heating element is stationary and the paper moves, thermal printers are mechanically simpler than inkjet or laser printers.

---

## Thermal Paper (Thermochromic Paper)

Standard paper cannot be used in a thermal printer — it won't react to heat.

**Thermal paper characteristics:**
- Coated with **thermochromic chemicals** that change color when heated
- Has a slightly **glossy texture** that distinguishes it from regular paper
- Changes to black/dark color when heat is applied

**Handling cautions:**

| Risk | Cause | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure to heat sources | Heat activates the coating | Paper turns black (ruined) |
| Clear tape | Chemical reaction with coating | Taped area turns white |

> **Exam Tip:** Thermal paper is sensitive to heat AND some adhesives. Storing receipts near a heat source (like a car dashboard) will darken them. Clear tape can bleach the coated area.
