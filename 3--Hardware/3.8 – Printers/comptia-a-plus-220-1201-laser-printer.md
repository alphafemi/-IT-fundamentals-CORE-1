## How a Laser Printer Works

The laser printing process follows these steps in order — commonly tested on the exam:

| Step | Process | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Charge (Processing)** | A **corona wire** or **charge roller** applies a uniform **negative charge** to the photosensitive drum (OPC drum) |
| 2 | **Expose (Writing)** | A **laser** writes the image onto the drum — wherever the laser touches, the negative charge is neutralized (discharged) |
| 3 | **Develop** | Negatively charged **toner** is attracted to the discharged (laser-written) areas of the drum; it repels from still-negative areas |
| 4 | **Transfer** | Paper passes by the drum; toner is transferred from the drum to the paper |
| 5 | **Fuse** | The paper passes through the **fuser** — heat and pressure permanently bond the toner to the paper |
| 6 | **Clean** | Remaining toner is scraped off the drum; the process repeats |

**Memory tip — "ECDTFC":** Charge → Expose → Develop → Transfer → Fuse → Clean

> **Exam Tip:** These six steps (and their order) are frequently tested. Know each step's name and what happens in it.

**Key components:**
- **Corona wire / charge roller** — applies initial negative charge to drum
- **OPC drum (Organic Photoconductor)** — photosensitive drum that holds the image
- **Toner** — negatively charged powder that creates the image on paper
- **Fuser** — heat + pressure unit that permanently bonds toner to paper

## Toner Cartridge

The toner cartridge contains the toner (and often the OPC drum).

**Toner configurations:**
- Some printers: OPC drum is **inside** the toner cartridge (replaced together)
- Some printers: OPC drum is **separate** from the toner cartridge

**Important handling rules:**
- Always stored in a **light-blocking bag** — keep in the bag until ready to install
- Never expose the OPC drum to light unnecessarily
- Power down the printer before replacing the cartridge
- Remove all packing strips and protective materials before installing

**Color laser printers:** Have **multiple cartridges** (cyan, magenta, yellow, black — CMYK), each in its own slot.

**Symptom of low toner:** Printed pages become progressively lighter.

## Maintenance Kit

Laser printers have many moving parts that wear out over time. Manufacturers provide a **maintenance kit** with replacement components.

**Typical contents:**
- Feed rollers
- Fuser unit
- Other model-specific wear parts

**When to use:**
- Most printers have a **page counter** — the manufacturer specifies the page count at which maintenance is due
- Check the page count via the printer's admin interface or web front-end

**Maintenance kit installation process:**
1. Power down the printer
2. Allow the fuser to cool before handling — **fusers get very hot**
3. Replace the components included in the kit
4. **Reset the page counter** after installation to track the next maintenance interval

## Calibration

After replacing a toner cartridge, output density may differ from the previous cartridge. **Calibration** corrects this.

- The printer prints **test pages** to evaluate toner density
- Settings allow adjustment of how much toner is applied per page
- Can be automated or manual depending on the printer model

## Cleaning & Safety

### Cleaning Method

| Area | Method |
|---|---|
| Exterior | Damp cloth with **cold water** |
| Interior (paper dust, toner) | Wipe away with cloth; use a **toner-specific vacuum** |
| Rollers | **Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA)** |

**Never use:**
- Compressed air inside the printer — launches toner particles into the air
- Harsh chemicals — can damage components
- Warm/hot water on toner spills — melts toner and makes it harder to remove

### Toner Safety

- Toner is a **very fine dust** — can become airborne easily
- If toner gets on skin: use **cold water** (not hot — heat melts toner)
- Be careful not to breathe toner dust; work in a ventilated area
- If toner spills inside the printer, wipe it up — do not blow with compressed air

### Fuser Safety

- The fuser reaches very high temperatures during operation
- Allow adequate cooling time before handling the fuser during maintenance
